Method

Principles I use to judge AI systems.

  • Evidence is part of the product, not an afterthought.
  • Human review should be designed as a control, not inserted as a vague fallback.
  • Arabic fluency is not enough; evaluation must test meaning, evidence, and decision behavior.
  • Agentic systems need session integrity: calls, arguments, state changes, permissions, and outputs must be replayable.
  • The best AI architecture reduces institutional uncertainty rather than hiding it behind a fluent interface.
