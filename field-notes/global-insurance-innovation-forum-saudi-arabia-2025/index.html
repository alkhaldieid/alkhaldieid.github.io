Global Insurance Innovation Forum Saudi Arabia 2025

From Buzzwords to Operational Change in Insurance

A keynote and executive panel on why insurance transformation succeeds only when data quality, change management, legacy integration, and AI controls are treated as one operating problem.

Date
Nov 10, 2025
Location
Saudi Arabia
Role
Keynote speaker and executive panelist on “From Data to Decision — AI in Action.”

The useful part of the forum was its refusal to stay at the level of polished transformation language. The discussion moved quickly toward the operational questions insurers face every day: fragmented execution, uneven data quality, legacy systems, customer frustration, and the gap between AI ambition and production reality.

My keynote focused on why many transformation programs fail despite strong intent. The problem is rarely a lack of vision alone. More often, priorities are misaligned, execution is fragmented, and change management is rushed before the organization has absorbed the operating consequences.

On the executive panel, the most valuable discussion centered on the path from data to decision. Better insurance AI depends on data quality from upstream entities, non-intrusive integration with existing systems, phased delivery that can finance itself, and AI use cases that shorten real claim cycles rather than decorate presentations.

The strongest signal from the audience was practical: transformation is credible when it reduces friction for customers, claims teams, underwriters, compliance teams, and IT teams at the same time. That requires less theatre and more operating discipline.

Discussion themes

  • Transformation fails when execution, governance, and change management are treated as separate workstreams.
  • Insurance AI must respect existing systems before it can improve them.
  • Data quality is not a back-office issue; it directly affects underwriting, claims, compliance, and customer trust.
  • Sentiment analysis, claims automation, and workflow intelligence matter only when they shorten cycles and improve service in measurable ways.
